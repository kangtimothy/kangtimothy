# Timothy Y. Kang

**Institutional AI Governance | Decision Authority | Formal Methods**

I research how institutions govern the use of artificial intelligence in consequential decisions, particularly how AI-generated contributions acquire decision weight and how authority, oversight, and accountability should be structured.

My work focuses on the intersection of:

- **Institutional Judgment Governance (IJG)** — judgment, reliance, and legitimate decision authority
- **Organizational Decision Governance (ODG)** — institutional oversight and accountability arrangements
- **Reliance Architecture (RA)** — operational mechanisms for governing human–AI reliance
- **Agentic and Embodied AI** — delegation, traceability, assurance, and control
- **Formal and Computational Methods** — modeling, evaluation, and reproducible research

My broader objective is to develop empirically grounded and technically implementable approaches to responsible institutional AI deployment.
